## [The Analysis of Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Price](https://ruffinellimarco.github.io/Portfolio/ev-charging-price.html)
* **[Read it in the browser](https://ruffinellimarco.github.io/Portfolio/ev-charging-price.html)** - no download required.
* The goal of this project is to understand how charging is priced on the basis of location and firm. Feel free to message me with questions pertaining to the analysis and data leveraged in this project on LinkedIn.
* Findings in brief: the largest effect on what a driver pays is the charging network itself (a 0.674 log-point spread between the most and least expensive operator), ahead of geography and facility type. Electricity, the largest variable cost in the business, passes through at an elasticity of only 0.156.
